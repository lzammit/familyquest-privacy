FamilyQuest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Contact: familyquestapp9@gmail.com

FamilyQuest (“the app”) is a family reward tracker. This policy explains what it collects, why, and your choices.

Who runs it

FamilyQuest is operated by Ludovic Zammit (Zammit Apps) as a personal project. There is no company behind it and no third-party advertising or analytics.

What the app collects

To sync one family’s data across that family’s own devices, the app stores, in Google’s Firebase (Firestore + Firebase Authentication):

DataWhyNotes
Google account identifier (email address, account user ID) To sign a parent in and identify which devices belong to a family Only for the adult who signs in.
Family members’ names and (optional) birthdays To show each member and their age May include children’s first names/ages entered by the parent.
Avatars and optional photos To personalize each member Cartoon avatars are settings; photos, if added, are stored as image data.
Goals and daily logs (check-offs, ratings, excused days) The core reward-tracking feature May relate to children.
Family settings (reward thresholds, theme, language, vacation mode) App configuration Family-wide.

The app contains no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

Children’s data

FamilyQuest is designed for parents to track young children’s weekly goals, so the data above may include information about children (first names, ages, goal history) that a parent chooses to enter. Children do not sign in or use accounts. Only the signed-in parent (and devices they explicitly add via a one-time code) can see a family’s data. Consistent with COPPA and similar laws, this information is used solely to provide the reward-tracking feature to that family and is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to build profiles.

Where it’s stored and who can see it

Data lives in the app’s Firebase project (Google Cloud). Access is restricted by security rules to the Firebase Authentication accounts on the family’s member list — i.e. the devices the family has signed in or paired. It is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. No one else, including the developer, uses this data for any other purpose.

Data sharing

Not sold. Not shared with third parties for advertising or analytics. The only processor is Google Firebase, acting as the storage/sync backend; see Google’s privacy policy for their handling.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete your account from inside the app (phone 1.3.1 and Fire TV 1.2.1 and later): Family Sync → Delete account… on the phone, Manage family → Delete account… on the TV. If your account is the only device left in the family, the family’s data is deleted with it.
  • Delete a member (and their goals/logs) from within the app at any time.
  • Sign out to stop syncing on a device.
  • If you no longer have the app installed, request deletion by emailing familyquestapp9@gmail.com — data will be removed from Firebase.

Full instructions, including what is deleted and what is retained: Delete your account and data.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
