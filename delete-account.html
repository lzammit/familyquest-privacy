Delete your FamilyQuest account and data

Apps: FamilyQuest (com.zammit.familyquest, Google Play) and FamilyQuest TV (com.zammit.familyquest.tv, Amazon Appstore) · Developer: Ludovic Zammit (Zammit Apps) · Last updated: 6 August 2026

You can delete your whole account from inside the app, delete just some of your data, or ask us to delete everything for you. All three routes are described below. You do not need an account to read this page.

Option 1 — delete your account in the app

Available in phone version 1.3.1 and Fire TV version 1.2.1 and later. If your copy has no Delete account… option, update the app, or use Option 3 below — email works from any version.

  1. Phone: tap the cloud icon at the top-left of the family screen to open Family Sync, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account…, then confirm.
  2. Fire TV: open Manage family, answer the adults-only question, then choose Delete account… (press again to confirm).

You may be asked to sign in again first: our sign-in provider requires a recent login before an account can be deleted. Your account is then deleted, and if it is the only device left in your family, the whole family's data is deleted with it. If other devices are still in the family, your account is removed and their copy of the family remains — use Option 3 if you want the entire family deleted.

Option 2 — delete only some data, keeping your account

Deleting a family member removes that member and everything recorded about them:

  1. Open FamilyQuest and tap the family member you want to remove.
  2. Open the member’s edit screen and choose Delete.
  3. Repeat for each member. This deletes their goals, daily check-offs and ratings, excused days, weekly results and any memory photos.

To stop syncing on a device without deleting anything, open Family Sync (the cloud icon at the top-left of the family screen) and tap Sign out.

Option 3 — ask us to delete everything

Email familyquestapp9@gmail.com from the account you signed in with (Google on the phone; the Fire TV app signs in without an account, so name the family in your message), with the subject “FamilyQuest deletion request”. We will delete your account and the whole family’s data. No other information is needed — sending the request from the signed-in address is how we identify the account.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, normally much sooner, and we reply to confirm once it is done.

What gets deleted, and what is kept

DataOn a deletion request
Family members’ names, birthdays, avatars and photos Deleted permanently
Goals, daily check-offs, ratings, excused days, weekly results, memory photos Deleted permanently
Family settings (reward thresholds, theme, language, vacation mode) Deleted permanently
Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication record: account user ID and the email address from Google Sign-In) Deleted permanently — the account itself is removed, not just its contents
Nothing is retained for advertising, analytics or profiling The app has no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, so no such data exists
Backups held by our backend provider (Google Firebase) Removed as those backups age out, within 90 days of the deletion

Deleting your Google account from FamilyQuest does not affect your Google account itself, which is managed by Google.
